Frequently Asked Questions About Stoli Vodka’s Russian Origins

Stoli Vodka is a premium vodka brand that has been in the market for over 80 years. It is famous for its smooth taste and high-quality ingredients, making it a favorite among vodka enthusiasts globally. However, with its Russian origins, Stoli often raises questions on its cultural roots and how they impact the brand’s identity. In this blog post, we answer some of the most frequently asked questions about Stoli Vodka’s Russian heritage.

What makes Stoli Vodka “Russian”?

Stolichnaya or “Stoli” was first produced in Moscow in 1938 by the state-owned distillery Mosshlemash. It then gained popularity within the Soviet Union, where its sales grew to make it one of Russia’s most famous vodka brands. Its original recipe remains unchanged to date. After the fall of communism, SPI Group, an independent company based in Luxembourg, took over Stoli’s global distribution rights from VVO Soyuzplodoimport.

Is Stoli Vodka still manufactured in Russia?

No. Following a legal dispute between SPI Group and Soyuzplodoimport over ownership rights to the brand name and label design worldwide, production moved out of Russia altogether to Latvia.
